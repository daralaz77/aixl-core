# blind27 (about 100 pairs, HUMAN-LABELLED data) — pre-registration, written 2026-11-06 BEFORE any pair is sampled or read
Purpose: the first test of the comparator, the judges and the strict hybrid on text and labels NOT written by Claude models. Every earlier set (blind19-26) had model authors, model labels and model judges of one family. Here the labels come from human annotators.
Data (downloaded with the user's authorisation, kept only in the session folder, never copied to the repository):
- PAWS-X, Spanish test split, 2,000 pairs (Hugging Face google-research-datasets/paws-x, read through the datasets-server JSON; parquet file 345,485 bytes also downloaded; license "other", not declared; cite Yang et al. 2019). label 1 = paraphrase, 0 = different meaning (EQUIVALENT / NOT_EQUIVALENT). Human-translated test set; the English originals were machine-generated and human-rated.
- CondaQA dev split, 1,110 rows, 115 passages (AbhilashaRavichander/CondaQA, Apache-2.0 code; dataset license file not read; cite Ravichander et al. 2022). PassageEditID 0 original, 1 paraphrase of the negated statement (EQUIVALENT), 2 scope edit (NOT_EQUIVALENT), 3 affirmative edit (NOT_EQUIVALENT), written by crowd workers. English only.
Raw hashes: condaqa_dev.json b9ec9ab7453fbdc61fbed988119c03f507341ac495f1f5f95b13b27f59e639a2; pawsx_es_test.parquet 5ba872e113a79c783616e59d3d28ec0e922b6db745d797e5eb95010a32928bee. I saw only the schema, the counts and ONE incidental PAWS-X row (the first row of a response) before writing this.

Sampling (fixed now, seed 27, automatic filters only, no manual selection):
- PAWS-X es: 30 random pairs with label 1 and 30 with label 0 among rows where both sentences have 4-45 words.
- CondaQA: for each PassageID take the distinct passage texts per PassageEditID; the pair is the sentences that differ between the original passage (edit 0) and the edited passage, cut at sentence boundaries; keep a candidate only if each side has 1-3 differing sentences and at most 60 words. Choose, with disjoint passages, 20 paraphrase pairs (0 vs 1), 10 scope-edit pairs (0 vs 2) and 10 affirmative-edit pairs (0 vs 3). If fewer are eligible, take all eligible.
Expected total 100 (50 EQUIVALENT, 50 NOT_EQUIVALENT); at most 1 false accept is allowed by G1.

Systems measured (identical to blind26): R0 comparator alone; R1 (PRIMARY) comparator EQUIVALENT AND both judges SAME (equal to the strict hybrid); the default hybrid; both judges SAME; each judge alone. Judges: two fresh agents (Opus, Sonnet), `arbiter_rules_v1.txt` verbatim, pairs anonymised and shuffled, no labels or source, one pass. Note: the arbiter prompt says "instructions"; these texts are statements. I keep the prompt verbatim for comparability and report the mismatch as a limit.
Code frozen as the working tree of aixl-core on 2026-11-06 (git HEAD 31696d7 plus the uncommitted strict-hybrid change); SHA-256 of the semantic files, hybrid_protocol.py, arbiter.py and the arbiter rules go into CODE_FREEZE.json before the judges run.

Gates:
  G1 (decides the claim): R1 false accepts <= 3% of the NOT_EQUIVALENT pairs, every id listed. VACUITY RULE (new, learned from blind26): if R1 proves fewer than 10 of the EQUIVALENT pairs, G1 for R1 is reported as VACUOUS, not PASS.
  Reported always: R0, default hybrid, both-judges and single-judge false accepts and proven-EQUIVALENT recall; error overlap between comparator and judges and between the two judges; table by source (PAWS-X es, CondaQA paraphrase / scope / affirmative).
Claim policy: no generalisation claim in any outcome. If G1 passes non-vacuously, the allowed sentence is: "on N human-labelled pairs from two public corpora, comparator AND two judges accepted k of M non-equivalent pairs", with recall next to it and the rule-of-three note. If it is vacuous or fails, say so.
Labels are the corpora's and are not corrected; known label noise in PAWS is listed, not fixed. One run; the evaluator refuses a second run; result written with an editor tool.
Expectation written beforehand: the comparator will prove fewer than 10 equivalent pairs (R1 VACUOUS), because the sentences are long encyclopedic statements outside its closed vocabulary. Both judges together will accept some non-equivalent PAWS-X pairs (3-15% false SAME) and some CondaQA scope-edit pairs. Judge errors will again be correlated. If R1 is VACUOUS the honest conclusion is that this test cannot validate the combination and only measures the judges and the comparator's fail-closed behaviour.
Honest limits: domain mismatch (encyclopedic statements, not agent instructions); PAWS non-paraphrases are word-order swaps that change who does what, close to our concern but not the same; judges are Claude models; CondaQA is English only; the two corpora's licenses are undeclared or unread, so nothing here is redistributed; about 100 pairs give a wide interval.
