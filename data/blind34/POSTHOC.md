# blind34 - post-hoc reading (NOT part of the pre-registered result; written after seeing it)

1. The authors' labels (Qwen2.5-32B and Mistral-Small-24B writing 'different' and 'same' pairs) are very noisy: a majority of the three judges contradicts the label on 138 of 954 pairs (122 of them labelled NOT_EQUIVALENT), mostly active / passive, 'except' / 'other than' / 'salvo', 'and' / 'but' and article changes. So the raw false-accept and false-SAME counts in RESULT_run1.txt (comparator 42 -> 40 -> 29 of 474; Claude pair 101 of 474) mostly count label noise, not errors.
2. Proxy that does not use the authors' labels: pairs the comparator calls EQUIVALENT while BOTH Claude judges say DIFFERENT. 0.7.1: 17 of 52 accepted; patch 0005: 17 of 50; 0.8.0 (frozen, pre-registered run): 6 of 39. Of the 13 pairs 0.8.0 stopped accepting, 11 are pairs both Claude judges call DIFFERENT. Three judges (adding Gemma-3-27B) all DIFFERENT: 7 -> 7 -> 1.
3. The 6 that remained were modal changes in one clause (must -> may; should / may swapped with must / should; PT deve -> pode) plus one tense change ('have paid' / 'had paid'). A guard for the modal class of a clause was added AFTER the run (0.8.1) and checked only on spent sets: 0.8.1 leaves 1 such pair (the tense one). That number is NOT a blind result.
4. Cross-family: Gemma-3-27B agrees with the Claude pair (both SAME or not) on 793 of 954 pairs; of the 130 NOT_EQUIVALENT-labelled pairs the Claude pair calls SAME, Gemma agrees SAME on 122 and DIFFERENT on 8. Those 122 are almost all label noise; the 8 are the cases where a second family adds protection.
5. Only one open judge ran (Gemma-3-27B); Phi-4 and Qwen3-32B were dropped for time (recorded in PREREG.md before the run).
