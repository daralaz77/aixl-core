R-noharm HOLDS (0 new false accepts, 0 lost) and R-safety HOLDS: neither version accepted any of the 63 non-EQUIVALENT-labelled pairs; G1 passes non-vacuously for both (0 of 63; R1-new proves 26, floor 10). The two judges alone accepted 6 of 63 non-equivalent pairs (9.5%), 3 of them in word_order_voice, and the comparator accepted none of them.
3. What still blocks the 14 unproven EQUIVALENT voice pairs (diagnosis AFTER the run, development evidence; W028 and X028 were traced, the rest are classified by pattern from the texts):
   - 5 are not active/passive pairs at all but fronting or clitic topicalisation ('Los informes de ventas, los firma ...', 'Na sexta-feira, a equipe envia ...', 'Ao final do dia, ...'): outside this rule.
   - 3 are Portuguese passives with 'pelo/pela' or a future ('são gerados ... pela equipe', 'registradas pelo sistema', 'será instalado pelo'): the parser does not read 'pelo/pela' as 'by', so the rule is inert in Portuguese.
   - 2 have a modal whose number differs ('debe aprobar' / 'deben ser aprobadas', 'deben revisar' / 'deben ser revisados'): the closed shape requires identical spelling; one of them also has 'action not recognized on one side'.
   - 1 is a preterite / past passive (X029 'instaló' / 'fue instalado'): excluded by design (simple present only).
   - 3 are English or Spanish present-tense pairs the rule DOES rewrite but that stay INCONCLUSIVE afterwards for reasons that were already in the comparator: W028 ('unresolved reference (do)' for the generic action 'update'), X028 (the dropped-feature ledger reports a plural mismatch because it counts the 3rd-person verb 'reviews' as a plural noun), Y001 ('every' in the subject).
4. Per the pre-registration ('a failing rule is reverted or narrowed'): the rule is NOT included in the patch handed to the user. It stays as a separate, unmerged patch for reference. The cost (about 70 lines in the comparator's hot path, a closed shape with eight guards) is not justified by +1 of 20 fresh pairs, even though it did no harm.
5. Two findings worth more than the rule itself. (a) An independent adversarial audit (259 pairs, a fresh agent with no stake in the rule) found 12 false accepts in the first version that neither the 14 guard cases I wrote nor the 360 spent pairs (0 new false accepts) had revealed; the closed-shape version had 0 of 259. An audit like this should be a standing step before freezing any new comparator rule. (b) `dropped_features` counts third-person verb forms ('approves', 'reviews') as plural nouns. This makes it blind to a real singular/plural noun difference in some active/passive pairs ('The manager approves the payment' vs 'The payments are approved by the manager' became EQUIVALENT with the first version of the rule) and, in other pairs, blocks a valid proof (X028). It is a reproducible lead independent of the voice rule.
