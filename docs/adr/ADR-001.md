# ADR-001: The semantic graph is the core; AIXL is one serialization
Status: Accepted

**Problem.** Comparing instructions by their wording (or by a compact string) mixes language, style and meaning (master prompt §2, §40, §96).

**Decision.** Meaning lives in `SemanticGraph` and its `canonical()` form (`aixl/core/semantic_graph.py`). AIXL (`aixl/serialization/aixl_codec.py`) and JSON (`json_codec.py`) are interchangeable encodings; comparison, diff, drift and fingerprint never read surface text or the AIXL line.

**Alternatives.** (a) compare AIXL strings; (b) compare embeddings of the text; (c) make AIXL the model.

**Reasoning.** (a) makes syntax changes look like meaning changes; (b) is not explainable and cannot say what was lost; (c) couples every consumer to one wire format.

**Consequences.** Anything the translator does not extract is invisible to every downstream engine (fingerprint, round trip), so translator quality bounds everything (LIMITATIONS #1). Every new semantic dimension needs graph, canonical, codec and comparator changes.

**Compatibility.** AIXL-0.2 and 0.3 lines still decode. Canonical form additions are keyed dimensions (see ADR-010).
