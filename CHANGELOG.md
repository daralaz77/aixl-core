# Changelog

Human-readable summary of what changes between releases. The detailed, per-change compatibility tables (backward compatibility, migration, test impact) are in [docs/SEMANTIC_CHANGELOG.md](docs/SEMANTIC_CHANGELOG.md); the project history is in [docs/HISTORY.md](docs/HISTORY.md). Version numbers follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/) (see `docs/VERSIONING_POLICY.md`); while the major version is 0, a MINOR bump may change the canonical form.

## Unreleased
Evidence only; no behaviour change. A comparator rule was tried and NOT merged (blind31).
### Comparator
- Fixed three classes of wrong EQUIVALENT that the published comparator produced, found by independent adversarial audits. Verdicts only move from EQUIVALENT to INCONCLUSIVE (never the reverse): (1) NEGATION SCOPE: 'Never save, but delete' no longer equals 'Save, but never delete', nor 'Never email the client and notify the manager' 'Email the client and never notify the manager' (each negation word gets a clause index; clause breaks are ':' ';' and connectors such as but / and / while / so / otherwise / because; prohibition words count as negation); (2) THE EXCEPTED SET: 'exceto o teste' / 'exceto os testes' and 'except the test servers' / 'except the test server' (the number of the excepted nouns is compared noun by noun and in order, and the number of every article or demonstrative after the exception word; more exception words are recognised for this check); (3) AN ANSWER: 'Keep logs? No, fewer than five days' no longer equals 'No fewer than five days' (a mark of any kind after a negation or answer word that is present in one text and absent in the other leaves the pair INCONCLUSIVE; a closing mark at the end of the text, and a parenthetical comma, are ignored).
- Measured: 0 of the 540 spent pairs lose a proof (135 proven before and after, the same 3 old false accepts). On three independent audits (about 1000 hand-written adversarial pairs) the pairs wrongly accepted fall from 124 to 5 of 210, from 191 to 15 of 274 and from 195 to 49 of 320, with no new false accept; the same audits lose 5 of 63, 8 of 62 and 19 of 91 equivalent proofs (mostly punctuation after a negation word and the order of a list of exceptions). The second and third audits were used to tune the closed lists, so only the first and the first pass of each count as independent. 1156 of 1806 generated negation-scope pairs were accepted before, 0 now.
- Closed the six open gaps and more (verdicts again only move from EQUIVALENT to INCONCLUSIVE): negation inside a relative clause ('the clients who have not paid'; a relative clause ends at the next modal, and a negation right before the modal belongs to the main verb), negation moved across a comma splice ('The file is not saved, the copy must be deleted'; a comma before a clause is a break, but the first comma after a front adjunct such as 'If ...,' / 'Please,' and a comma before a connector or 'nor' are not), same-clause scope after ':', 'but that / but those', the article after 'com exceção da / das', 'excepción hecha', 'a não ser', 'a menos que' and 'fora'. Also: 'whether' opens a clause; negative quantifiers (ningún, nenhum, none) count as negation in the layout; 'Don't, wipe the cache' is an answer unless a parenthetical adverb follows; an answer word keeps its mark when it is quoted ('no, fewer ...'); 'yes / yeah / sim / tampouco / negativo' are answer words.
- Measured: the 540 spent pairs still prove 135 (0 lost, same 3 old false accepts). On the three earlier audits (801 pairs) the wrongly accepted pairs go from 69 to 9, no new false accept, no equivalent proof lost (two more proved). The earlier audits were used to tune these rules, so those numbers are optimistic. A fourth, independent audit (545 non-equivalent, 201 equivalent, written blind): accepted 16 -> 5, equivalents proved 91 -> 93, and it found one regression of this change ('No, más de diez' = 'No más de diez', because 'mas' was treated as a connector), fixed and covered by a test.
- Slot binding (blind33 round; verdicts again only move from EQUIVALENT to INCONCLUSIVE): when both texts have the same actions but a weekday, clock time, today / tomorrow, number, bound ('at least / up to'), 'before / after / within' or modal (must / may / should / can, en-es-pt) hangs on a different action, the pair is refused ('Back up on Monday and migrate on Tuesday' / the swap; 'must back up and may restart' / the swap; 'at least 3 copies and at most 5 minutes' / the swap). Line breaks and spaced dashes count as clause breaks for this check. Negation in a relative clause is now told apart from the main clause's ('Students who do not cheat pass' / 'Students who cheat do not pass'); 'but the test servers' number, and an exception phrase that opens one text and closes the other with several actions, are refused.
- Closed-shape rewrites (these ADD proofs; each one has a guard and a test): 'which / who' = 'that' before an auxiliary and after a non-verb word, 'have to / tienes que / tem que' = must (never after a negation or another modal: 'do not have to' is not 'must not'), 'N or more / N or fewer / N at most' and 'a minimum of' = the prefix bounds, ', but <clause>' = '; <clause>' (only before an auxiliary, pronoun or action verb), 'except when / except if' = 'unless'.
- blind33 (270 fresh pairs from three authors who never saw the code; pre-registered; one three-way paired run: published 0.7.1 / patch 0005 / this change, `data/blind33/RESULT_run1.txt`): false accepts 11 of 135 -> 8 -> 0. R-safety and R-binding HOLD. R-recall does NOT hold (equivalents proven 3 -> 2 -> 1 of 135, needed +3 over patch 0005) and R-loss holds (1 lost, 'Todos deben fichar, excepto X' / 'Salvo X, todos deben fichar', fixed after the run together with 'except when'; the fixes were checked only on spent sets, so they are not a blind result). The closed-shape rewrites therefore did not raise recall on fresh authors: 61 of 128 unproven equivalents differ in words with no known synonym, which a rule list cannot cover. Proven equivalents stay at about 2% on open-domain paraphrases: this comparator remains a loss detector, not an equivalence prover.
- End-to-end hybrid on blind33 (two fresh judges, `data/blind33/hybrid/`, spent set so evidence only): SAME on 117 of 135 equivalents with this change (115 with 0.7.1 and with patch 0005), 0 false SAME of 135 non-equivalents in all three; the judges alone give 134 / 0. The rest of the equivalents go to REVIEW because of the comparator's veto (9 suspected differences, 8 ambiguity flags). On these pairs the veto added no measured safety and cost recall; the earlier evidence for its value is in docs/EVIDENCE.md.
- `hybrid.decide(..., veto='all'|'definite')` and `hybrid_protocol.decide(..., veto=...)` (opt-in, default unchanged): with 'definite' only a definite NOT_EQUIVALENT of the comparator sends a unanimous SAME to REVIEW; a suspected difference or an AMBIGUOUS_* flag no longer does. Measured with two fresh judges (spent sets, `data/blind33/hybrid/RESULT_veto.md`): SAME on equivalents 117 -> 129 of 135 (blind33) and 110 -> 112 of 120 (adversarial), false SAME unchanged (0 of 135; 3 of 240, the same three in both settings, labels arguable). The veto caught 0 judge errors in 630 pairs; the second judge, not the veto, is what makes SAME safe (Haiku alone said SAME on 18 % of the not-equivalent adversarial pairs, 2-of-2 on 1.3 %).
- Dev evidence after the change (spent sets, not blind): 540 spent pairs 135 proven before and after; the three earlier audits 9 wrongly accepted pairs before and after; audit 5 (written blind) 222 -> 0 wrongly accepted, equivalents proven 93 -> 112 (the rewrite shapes were chosen from that audit's list, so that gain is not independent).
- Still open: the KIND of mark after an answer word is not meaning (design decision, tested as a control); 'each / every' on the subject proven equal on purpose (blind30, `KNOWN_OPEN` in the mutation test); a contrapositive reading of a relative clause is now refused instead of accepted.

Rule not merged (the reference patch is kept outside the tree).
- An independent adversarial audit (259 pairs) found 12 false accepts in the first version of the rule that the author's own guards and the 360 spent pairs had missed; they are now `tests/test_semantic_voice_guards.py` (23 guard pairs, all unproven on v0.7.1).
- `data/blind32` (60 fresh bounds pairs, pre-registered, one paired run main vs one lexicon entry 'no fewer than' = 'at least'): no new false accept, but the pre-registered recall rule (+2) does NOT hold: +1 (16 to 17 of 30). Entry not merged. An independent audit of 440 pairs also removed two other candidate entries ('jamais'; 'com exceção de') before the test.
- `tests/test_semantic_known_false_accepts.py`: seven pairs the PUBLISHED comparator wrongly calls EQUIVALENT (negation scope 'Never save, but delete' = 'Save, but never delete'; the number of an excepted set; a 'No,' answer glued to a bound), as strict xfail; `tests/test_semantic_lexicon_guards.py`: 18 guard pairs for future lexicon entries.
- Lead for a future round: `dropped_features` counts third-person verb forms ('approves', 'reviews') as plural nouns; it can mask a real singular/plural difference and can block a valid proof.

## 0.7.1 — 2026-11-11
One comparator rule and its evidence (blind30). Wire token stays `V:AIXL-0.3`. Some pairs that were INCONCLUSIVE now come out EQUIVALENT (never the reverse), so this is a PATCH-sized change by the rule that only adds proofs.
### Comparator
- 'each / every / cada / todo' and 'all / todos' on the SUBJECT are now proven equal when nothing is counted, summed, excepted or ordered (no number, no collective word such as 'together' or 'total', no 'except', no 'before / after / antes'); exactly one plural carried by the quantified subject is not a difference. An object quantifier, a number or an order marker keep the old behaviour (a difference is only suspected). The mutation property test records the one case it changes in `KNOWN_OPEN` as a deliberate policy change.
- Measured on 120 fresh pairs (`data/blind30`, v0.7.0 against the new code on the same pairs): 2 more equivalent pairs proven in the targeted constructs (4 to 6 of 26), no false accept in either version, nothing lost elsewhere. The pre-registered rule asked for +5 and does NOT hold. A second rule written in the same round (a passive with a modal rewritten to the active sentence) fired on no fresh pair and was reverted.
- Evidence set `data/blind30` (120 pairs, two over-sampled constructs, paired old-versus-new, pre-registered, one run).

## 0.7.0 — 2026-11-11
Strict hybrid, three comparator changes, and the evidence behind them. The wire token stays `V:AIXL-0.3`. Comparator verdicts change in a few pairs (see Comparator below), so this is a MINOR bump (`docs/VERSIONING_POLICY.md`).
### Added
- Strict hybrid: `aixl.semantic.hybrid_decide(..., require_semantic_equivalent=True)`, `hybrid_protocol.decide(..., strict=True)` and the MCP tool `aixl_hybrid_decide(..., strict=True)`. A unanimous judge SAME is accepted only if the rule-based comparator also proves EQUIVALENT; otherwise REVIEW. Default behaviour is unchanged. Measured evidence and its limits: `docs/HYBRID_MCP.md`.
- Evidence sets `data/blind26` (80 model-written pairs, new families) and `data/blind27` (100 human-labelled pairs from PAWS-X and CondaQA; references only, no third-party text), each pre-registered and run once.
- Evidence sets `data/blind28` (120 in-vocabulary pairs, three judges including a local non-Claude model) and `data/blind29` (120 fresh pairs, paired old-versus-new comparison).

### Comparator
- Fixed deadline phrasings are now proven equal: 'no later than X' = 'by X', 'by X at the latest' = 'by X', 'no máximo até' = 'até', 'no después del' = 'hasta el'. A bare 'at the latest' stays content. Applied after the strict-mode token identity, so strict mode still reads the words as written.
- A fronted time or frequency adjunct is moved to the end ('After C, do A' = 'Do A after C', 'Mañana, X', 'Cada semana, X', 'Every Monday, X'); 'before' and 'after' stay distinct.
- 'never / nunca / jamás' and 'qualquer / cualquier' on one side only now make EQUIVALENT impossible (INCONCLUSIVE).
- Measured on 120 fresh pairs (blind29, same pairs through the old and the new comparator): false accepts 2 -> 1 of 60 (none new), proven equivalents 29 -> 31 of 60 (3 gained, 1 lost; the pre-registered rule asked for +5 and does NOT hold). The gain is in the deadline construct (0/6 -> 3/6); the fronted-adjunct rule did not fire on any fresh pair. In-distribution constructs, model authors, small sample: not a generalisation claim.

## 0.6.0 — 2026-11-06
### Security
- Private vulnerability reporting, Dependabot, secret scanning and push protection enabled; `SECURITY.md` and `.well-known/security.txt` updated.
- CodeQL, OpenSSF Scorecard and ClusterFuzzLite (Atheris) run in CI; every GitHub Action, the base images and the Python dependencies are pinned by hash.
- Fixed a denial of service: `to_semantic` took 19 s on 2,000 digits (cubic regular expression) and a 16,000-digit number raised `ValueError`; both now finish in under a second.
- `docker-publish` runs only for pushes to this repository's own `main`.
- No known vulnerability is open. No CVE has been assigned to any issue fixed in this version.

### Comparator
- Closed list of difference types that may assert NOT_EQUIVALENT; unreliable ones are kept as `Verdict.suspected`.
- Dropped-feature ledger: plural, indefinite article, past/future marker and version-like numerals can no longer be silently folded away by EQUIVALENT.
- New strict mode: `compare_texts(a, b, strict=True)`, MCP tool `aixl_compare_semantic`, CLI `semantic --strict` (EQUIVALENT only for the same token sequence).
- Fixed 10 silent-failure holes found by outside-author evaluation sets (blind19–25) and by mutation properties.

### Added
- Mutation property tests, Hypothesis fuzzing, SBOM generation (`scripts/make_sbom.py`), `CONTRIBUTING.md`, security documentation (`docs/THIRD_PARTY.md`, `docs/INCIDENT_RESPONSE.md`, `docs/SECURITY_MILESTONES.md`).
